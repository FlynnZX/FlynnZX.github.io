# Translator notes (demo)
| Line | Choice made | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 顺路而已 | "I was in the area." | Literal "it's on my way" breaks the joke set up next; "in the area" lets her rebut with east/west. |
| 城市是圆的 | "The city's round." | Kept the deadpan non-logic; it is the character's voice. |
| 算你会说话 | "I'll give you that one." | Literal "you're good at talking" sounds flat; this keeps her teasing concession. |
| 《边城》 | Border Town | Shen Congwen's classic (published 1934); standard English title, no footnote needed in-game. |
| 退货 | "I'm returning it." | Shop-clerk joke kept: she works in a bookshop. |
| Choice 2 | "I'd never want to finish it." | Shortened to fit the choice-button width; meaning (reluctance to end) preserved. |
| Names | Lin Xia / Chen Mo | Pinyin, family name first, no tone marks; glossary entry created for consistency. |

Workflow: script extraction (Ren'Py `translate` files) > glossary + style sheet > draft > native-English edit > in-engine QA (line length, text overflow, fonts) > delivery as tl/english/.
